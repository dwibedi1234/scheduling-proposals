01Proposal in brief

Scheduling work rarely arrives evenly — a new baseline, a month-end update cycle and a recovery schedule can all land in the same week. I offer flexible, project-based Primavera P6 support from India: an extra scheduler who works to your team’s standards, on a freelance or subcontract basis.

This sheet sets out what I can take off your desk, how the working day fits between the US and India, and a low-risk way to start: one small assignment, so you can judge the work before committing to anything longer.
